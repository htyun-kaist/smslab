ME Springboard launched on 2025 Fall

September 3, 2025

SMS lab launches ME Springboard program in this semester. Five students from ME will work on developing flexible robotic bin picking system. They will learn how to integrate hardware like depth camera and robot into image processing, measurement and control, and deep learning.
